# Dun & Bradstreet (D&B)

> Dun & Bradstreet (D&B) is a global provider of business data, analytics, and insights, helping organizations manage risk, drive sales and marketing, assess supplier and third-party risk, and analyze finance and credit — built on the D&B Data Cloud, covering hundreds of millions of business records worldwide.

This is the root index for AI agents, LLMs, and crawlers accessing D&B websites. It links to per-locale `llms.txt` files — each a list of canonical page URLs with short descriptions — plus cross-market directories and a guide for retrieving clean page content.

D&B content commonly covers: business identity, the D-U-N-S Number, business credit, risk management, compliance, supplier and third-party risk, sales and marketing intelligence, master data, and AI-ready business data.

## How to use this file

- Pick the `llms.txt` for your target market and language from **Site index** below.
- Fetch that file to get canonical page URLs, each with a short description.
- To pull clean page content, see **Retrieving page content** for Markdown content negotiation and page-structure notes.
- Follow canonical URLs, respect robots directives, caching headers, and rate limits.

## Retrieving page content

D&B pages are server-side rendered (SSR): the initial HTML contains core content, metadata, navigation, footer links, images, CTAs, and structured data before client-side JavaScript runs.

- **Markdown via content negotiation.** Request any page with `Accept: text/markdown` to get a clean Markdown representation better suited to retrieval, summarization, and indexing than rendered HTML. Example: `curl -H "Accept: text/markdown" https://www.dnb.com/en-us/`
- **AEM page model.** The page source includes a `window.__AEM_PAGE_MODEL__` object mirroring the authored AEM content model — page fields (`metatitle`, `metadescription`, `pageType`, `language`, `title`, `templateName`, `lastModifiedDate`, `replicationDate`) and a component tree under `:items` / `:itemsOrder`, with authored text, headings, CTA labels/URLs, cards, tags, and media.
- **JSON-LD.** `<script type="application/ld+json">` blocks in the head provide schema.org data (e.g., Organization, WebPage, BreadcrumbList) for additional semantic context.
- **Precedence.** Prefer the Markdown representation when available; fall back to SSR HTML as the baseline source of truth; use `__AEM_PAGE_MODEL__` to understand component hierarchy and authored content beyond the DOM.
